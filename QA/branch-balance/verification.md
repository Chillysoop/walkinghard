# Astral chart and approved branch balance — 0.9.3

The Office is now one navigable chart containing all 36 existing unlocks. Six outer constellations and the inner Foundations connect through the Office hub. Solid edges represent actual prerequisites; dotted entry paths introduce no new purchase gate. Drag empty space, zoom around the pointer, focus a branch, or use Fit Chart / Home. Click a circular node to select its details; only the explicit Unlock control spends Sparks. Tab / Shift+Tab cycles nodes. Selection traces prerequisites; lock, cost and check badges distinguish state. Neutral/cyan outlines are independent of item rarity. No third-party art is used for the chart; item art is the existing catalog artwork.

All 228 Sparks of costs, original unlock identities, profile schema, seal gates, and frozen saved-journey pools are retained. Native screenshots cover every branch at 1280×720, 1920×1080 and 2560×1080, plus whole-chart, affordable, purchased and zoomed views. The existing reduced-motion preference suppresses expanding purchase rings. Representative images are in `captures/`; the full native set remains in ignored `QA/v093-astral-native`.

## Applied tuning

- Static Shroud grants a 3-second barrier only after at least one successful loop mark. Capacity is 6 / 6.9 / 7.8 / 8.7 / 9.6. The same armor instance refreshes rather than stacks; the shared 90-point barrier cap remains. Empty loops, out-of-range targets and consumed-origin mark rejection do not grant protection. Attribution, last-wave feedback and guard synergies use the actual host.
- Breach Salvo base damage is 28 instead of 25. Duration remains 0.8s, lane target cap three, and the prior-stagger bonus 50%.
- Rivet stays 20; rifle stays 42. No other item, route, boss, health, physics or 4× rules were tuned.

## Matched comparisons

`engine-results.jsonl.gz` retains 156 valid outcomes: 144 Fast and 12 Full PhysX. Both sides use the same candidate executable; `baseline.json` disables Shroud's new barrier and scales Salvo back to 25. Exact kits, slots, equal rarities and seeds are in `fast-cases.json` and `full-cases.json`. Each side has 48 primary Fast cases (Storm/Ballistics × Common/Rare/Legendary × eight seeds) and 24 cross-build cases (Impact with Shroud, Sever with Salvo × three rarities × four seeds). Each Full side has two Rare seeds per primary kit and one Common seed per kit.

These are fixed-kit eight-wave encounters with normal starting health, no further loot and checkpoint-three ultimate progression. They are **not ordinary 21-encounter player win rates**. Fast and Full are kept separate. HP includes zero for deaths and is not normalized to gear health. No invalid outcomes, missing/duplicate cases or timeouts occurred. Full confirmations remain small samples.

The approved Salvo increase is retained as a modest damage change; these results do **not** demonstrate a reliable survival improvement. Its lethal-hit timing changes crowd evolution, so higher damage need not yield higher ending HP. No further increase is warranted from this sample.
